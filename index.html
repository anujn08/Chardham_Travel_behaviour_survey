How to read Comfort and Reliability

Comfort

◔ Low Example: crowded bus, standing, long walk, or physically tiring travel
◑ Medium Example: seat available, manageable crowding, some effort or transfers
● High Example: comfortable seat/cabin, easier boarding, less physical strain

Reliability

! Low Example: likely delay, service uncertainty, cancellation, closure, or long waiting
~ Medium Example: generally available, but some waiting or delay may happen
✓ High Example: more predictable timing, fewer disruptions, dependable availability
